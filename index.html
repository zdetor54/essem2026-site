European Summer School on Eye Movements (ESSEM 2026)

ESSEM brings together internationally renowned researchers to teach students of all levels in the scientific foundations of eye movement research and the design and analysis of eye movement studies in basic and applied settings.

ESSEM 2026 - Key Info
  • Dates: 7-12 September, 2026
  • Location: The Institute of Psychiatry, Psychology & Neuroscience (IoPPN), King’s College London
  • Course fee: £560 (includes the registration fee, lunch & tea/coffee breaks during the workshop, and the ESSEM 2026 group dinner).
